Source: https://see.stanford.edu/

## Vosk speech model

The APK bundles `vosk-model-small-cn-0.22` from Alpha Cephei under Apache License 2.0.
The archive is extracted into app-private storage on first launch and is not downloaded
at runtime.

Source: https://alphacephei.com/vosk/models

The bundled archive checksum and notice are recorded in
`app/src/main/assets/models/NOTICE.txt`.
